# Assignment 02: AI vs ML vs DL Visual Essay

**Hello Class:**

The objective of this assignment is to demonstrate your understanding of the differences between **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Machine Learning (ML)**, and **Deep Learning (DL)** through a visual and conceptual comparison using real-world STEM examples.

## Instructions

1. **Create a visual essay** (infographic or slide deck) that explains:
   - What is AI?
   - What is ML?
   - What is DL?
   - How are they different and how do they relate?

2. **Include at least one real-world STEM example** for each:
   - **AI Example:** AI in climate prediction models
   - **ML Example:** ML in medical diagnostics
   - **DL Example:** DL in autonomous vehicles or robotics

3. **Add engaging visuals and layout:**
   - You may use **Canva**, **Google Slides**, **PowerPoint**, or any design tool of your choice.
   - Include diagrams, icons, and/or images to support your explanations.

4. **Export your final work as a:**
   - **Microsoft Word document (.doc/.docx)** OR
   - **PDF file (.pdf)**

   *(Tip: If using Google Slides or Canva, you can download/export your work as a PDF.)*

5. **Submission Format Requirements:**
   - At the top of your file, include the following information:
     - Your Full Name
     - Course Title: [Insert Course Name]
     - Instructor: [Insert Instructor's Name]
     - Date: [Insert Date]
     - Assignment Title: AI vs ML vs DL Visual Essay
   - At the bottom of your document, include a **"Resources Used"** section listing:
     - All websites, tools, articles, or books you used to gather your information and visuals
     - Full titles and/or hyperlinks
