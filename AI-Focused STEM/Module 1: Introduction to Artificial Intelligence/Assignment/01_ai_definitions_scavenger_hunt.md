# Assignment 01: AI Definitions Scavenger Hunt

**Hello Class:**

The **objective** of this assignment is for you to begin exploring basic Artificial Intelligence (AI) vocabulary by finding and defining key terms using **Open Educational Resources (OER)**.

## Instructions

1. **Choose 5 important AI terms** that you’ve encountered in this module. Examples include:
   - Algorithm
   - Neural Network
   - Intelligent Agent
   - Machine Learning
   - Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

   *(You may choose other relevant terms if approved by the instructor.)*

2. **Define each term** in your own words using reliable **Open Educational Resources (OER)** such as:
   - [Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com/)
   - [AI4ALL Open Curriculum](https://ai-4-all.org/)
   - [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)
   - [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)

3. **Create a table** (recommended) or a slide deck that includes:
   - **Term**
   - **Definition**
   - **Include the Source (with hyperlink if possible)**

4. **Submit your assignment** as either:
   - A **Microsoft Word document (.doc or .docx)**, or
   - A **PDF file (.pdf)**

5. **Submission Format Requirements:**
   - Your Word or PDF file must include the following at the top of the document:

     - Your Full Name
     - Course Title: [Insert Course Name, e.g., Introduction to AI for STEM Students]
     - Instructor: [Insert Instructor’s Name]
     - Date: [Insert Date]
     - Assignment Title: AI Definitions Scavenger Hunt

   - At the bottom of the document or slides, include a **“Resources Used”** section listing all the websites or materials you used, with URLs or full titles.

*This assignment follows a [grading rubric](01_ai_definitions_scavenger_hunt_rubric.md).*
