# Sample Answers to Module 1: Evaluation Questions

## Short Answer Questions

### 1. In your own words, define Machine Learning. Provide one example of how it is used in a STEM field.

**Sample Answer:** Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data. In healthcare, ML can detect diseases like cancer by analyzing medical images.

### 2. Describe what an intelligent agent is. Give an example from a real-world system.

**Sample Answer:** An intelligent agent perceives its environment through sensors and acts through actuators to achieve a goal. For example, a Roomba vacuum navigates and cleans based on its environment.

### 3. What is one ethical challenge that arises from using AI in hiring or admissions?

**Sample Answer:** One challenge is algorithmic bias. If training data reflects past inequalities, AI may unfairly favor or disfavor certain groups, leading to discrimination.

### 4. List two differences between Machine Learning and Deep Learning.

**Sample Answer:** ML uses algorithms that need feature selection by humans; DL uses neural networks that learn features automatically. DL requires more data and computing power than ML.

## Scenario-Based Questions

### 1. Scenario-Based Question

**Scenario:** A self-driving car must choose between hitting a pedestrian or swerving into a wall, potentially harming the driver.

**Question:** Discuss this from an AI ethics perspective. What principles should guide this decision, and who should be accountable?

**Sample Answer:** Ethical frameworks like utilitarianism or the precautionary principle can guide the decision. Developers must ensure transparency and fairness in decision logic. Responsibility may fall on designers, manufacturers, and regulatory bodies.

### 2. Scenario-Based Question

**Scenario:** A school uses AI to automatically assign grades based on student behavior and performance data.

**Question:** What are some ethical concerns with this approach?

**Sample Answer:** Concerns include lack of transparency, potential bias in grading, over-reliance on non-academic data, and students not having the ability to challenge grades.

### 3. Scenario-Based Question

**Scenario:** A facial recognition system in a public space is used for law enforcement.

**Question:** What are two potential benefits and two ethical risks of this use of AI?

**Sample Answer:** Benefits: faster identification of suspects, public safety. Risks: invasion of privacy, misidentification—especially among minority groups.

### 4. Scenario-Based Question

**Scenario:** A company uses an AI chatbot to replace human customer service agents.

**Question:** What are the potential social and economic impacts of this decision?

**Sample Answer:** Economically, it reduces costs, but may lead to job losses. Socially, customers may face frustration if the bot cannot understand complex requests.
