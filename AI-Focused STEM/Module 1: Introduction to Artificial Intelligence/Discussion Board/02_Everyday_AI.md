# Discussion Board 02: Everyday AI

**Hello class:**

The goal of this discussion is to help you recognize how Artificial Intelligence (AI) already impacts your daily life or your chosen STEM field, even if you don’t realize it. This activity also helps you build communication and critical thinking skills by sharing insights and engaging with others.

## Instructions

### Step 1 – Create Your Post (by [Insert Due Date])

Write a post (**at least 150 words**) where you:

1. **Describe one way AI impacts your life or your STEM field of interest.**
   - This can be something simple like using facial recognition on your phone, or something field-specific like how AI is used in climate modeling, medical imaging, agriculture, robotics, etc.
2. **Explain how it works at a basic level (if known).**
   - If you’re not sure, take an educated guess based on what you’ve learned so far or do a quick search using a credible source.
3. **Reflect on its usefulness.**
   - Is AI making life or work easier? Are there drawbacks or concerns?

### Step 2 – Reply to Classmates (by [Insert Reply Due Date])

Respond to **at least two peers** with thoughtful, respectful replies:

- Ask a question
- Share a related experience
- Expand on their idea or suggest a new angle

*Replies should be at least 3–4 sentences long.*

## Tips for Success

- Don’t worry about being an AI expert — just share your perspective!
- Be respectful in your responses.
- If you're not sure how AI works in your example, focus on why it matters to you.
