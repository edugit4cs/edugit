# Discussion Board: AI in Popular Culture

Helo class:

This discussion is designed to help you reflect on how AI is portrayed in popular culture and how those portrayals differ from the real-world uses of AI we will explore in this module.

### Instructions

1. Think about a movie, show, book, game, or comic that featured Artificial Intelligence (AI).
   - Examples include *The Matrix*, *Iron Man*, *Ex Machina*, *Big Hero 6*, or even *WALL·E*.
2. In your post, answer the following:
   - **What AI character or technology did you see?**
   - **What could it do?**
   - **Was it portrayed as helpful, dangerous, emotional, human-like, etc.?**
   - **Do you think this is a realistic example of AI based on what you know? Why or why not?**
3. After you post, reply to **at least two classmate's posts** with a thoughtful comment or question. You may:
   - Compare their AI example with yours
   - Ask if they think their example could ever exist in real life
   - Share additional facts or thoughts

**Deadline:** Post your response by **[Insert Date]** and your reply by **[Insert Date + 2 days]**

Remember to keep your tone respectful and curious. We're here to learn from each other!
