# Discussion Board 03: Ethics in AI

**Hello class:**

This discussion invites you to think critically about the ethical implications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in situations where human lives may be at stake. You will explore your own values and reasoning while learning from the diverse perspectives of your classmates.

## Instructions

### Step 1 – Create Your Post (by [Insert Due Date])

Write a post (**at least 200 words**) responding to the following prompt:

**Do you believe AI should make life-or-death decisions?**  
(For example: In medicine, autonomous vehicles, military drones, or disaster response systems.)

Your post must include:

1. **Your clear position** – Yes, No, or “It depends” (and explain what it depends on).
2. **Support your opinion with a real-world example**
   - This can be from an article, a current event, a case study, or your own experience.
3. **Discuss any ethical concerns**
   - Consider topics like bias, accountability, transparency, fairness, or unintended consequences.

### Step 2 – Reply to Classmates (by [Insert Reply Due Date])

Respond to **at least two peers** with respectful, thoughtful replies:

- Do you agree or disagree with their position? Why?
- Can you offer a different perspective or counterexample?
- Ask a follow-up question that pushes the discussion further.

*Each reply should be at least **3–4 sentences** long.*

## Tips for Success

- Be honest and thoughtful — there's no “right” answer.
- Use respectful language when responding to peers, especially if you disagree.
- Feel free to link to a current article or example if you’d like to support your opinion.
