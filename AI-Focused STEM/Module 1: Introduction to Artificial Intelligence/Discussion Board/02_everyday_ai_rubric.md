# Discussion 02: Everyday AI — Grading Rubric

**Module:** 1  
**Points Possible:** 30  
**Criteria:** 3 (10 points each)

---

## 1. Original Post (10 points)

You provided a clear and well-thought-out example of how AI is used in your daily life or field of study. Your post meets the required word count and shows effort in explaining your ideas.

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Clear, well-developed example of AI; meets word count; shows thought and effort | 10 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | Adequate example but lacks depth or word count | 6 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | Vague or incomplete example; lacks effort | 3 pts |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |


## 2. Critical Thinking (10 points)

You explained how the AI in your example works (or made a reasonable guess) and reflected on its impact—how it helps, changes, or influences what we do or experience.

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Reflects on how AI works and its impact clearly | 10 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | Some attempt to analyze impact or function | 6 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | Little or no reflection | 3 pts |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |


## 3. Peer Replies (10 points)

You responded to at least two classmates with meaningful and engaging comments. You added to the discussion by asking questions, sharing your perspective, or offering new insights.

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Two or more thoughtful, engaging replies | 10 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | One basic reply or two brief responses | 6 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | Minimal engagement | 3 pts |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |
