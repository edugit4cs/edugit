# Discussion 01: AI in Popular Culture — Grading Rubric

**Module:** 1  
**Points Possible:** 15  
**Criteria:** 3 (5 points each)

---

## 1. Original Post (5 points)

Clearly described an example of AI from a movie, show, book, or game. Student included what it could do and explained how it was portrayed (helpful, scary, emotional, etc.).

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Clearly describes an AI example with thoughtful comparison to real AI | 5 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | Describes AI example with basic comparison | 3 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | Incomplete or vague description; little effort shown | 1 pt |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |


## 2. Critical Thinking (5 points)

Student gave an opinion about whether the movie example is a realistic use of AI and explained why, based on current knowledge about real-world AI.

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Explains why the example is or isn’t realistic with clear reasoning | 5 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | Some attempt to analyze realism of AI | 3 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | No analysis or misconceptions present | 2 pts |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |


## 3. Peer Response (5 points)

Student replied to at least two classmate’s posts with a meaningful comment. Student added to the conversation by sharing an idea, asking a question, or making a thoughtful comparison.

| Rating | Performance Level | Description | Points |
|:------:|-------------------|-------------|------:|
| 3 | Excellent | Responds thoughtfully with a question, comparison, or new idea | 5 pts |
| 2 | Satisfactory | Responds with a basic comment or agreement | 3 pts |
| 1 | Needs Improvement | No reply or reply lacks substance | 2 pts |
| 0 | No Marks | No post | 0 pts |
