# AI Education for STEM and Non-STEM Students

Supported by the State Council of Higher Education for Virginia (SCHEV)

## About This Course

This open educational resource introduces artificial intelligence
concepts and applications to both STEM and non-STEM learners.

The curriculum combines foundational AI concepts, hands-on activities,
generative AI, machine learning, responsible AI, and interdisciplinary
applications.

## Course Tracks

### STEM Track

For students interested in computing, engineering, data science,
and related disciplines.

### Non-STEM Track

For students from humanities, business, education, social sciences,
and other disciplines.
