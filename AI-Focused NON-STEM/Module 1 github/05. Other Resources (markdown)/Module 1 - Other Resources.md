## Module 1 Course Materials

### READINGS

In the following readings, you’ll learn:

- What is AI Really?
- Explore the difference between pop culture AI and real-world AI
- How AI is already part of your daily life (even if you don’t realize it)
- Compare popular media images of AI (movies, news) to real-life AI applications
- Reflect on ethical concerns and the impact of AI on society - ethical questions AI raises in society
